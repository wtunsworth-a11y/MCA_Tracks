# Track recording checklist - Managalas Conservation Area

Generated by `scripts/cross_reference_tracks.py` from the team's list in
`data/reference/planned_tracks.csv` against the GPS recordings held in
`data/processed/tracks.gpkg`. Re-run it after each new drop of recordings.

A leg counts as recorded only when both its ends sit within 500 m of a
recorded line **and** the recorded network routes between them without a
long detour. Matching names were not enough to pass a leg: most of the
recordings in hand carry no route name at all.
